1. Navigating AliRemote & Selecting Your Phone

  • Open AliRemote and click 'iPhones' in the left sidebar to see your assigned Cell phones (e.g., Elsa - 1 through Elsa - 5).
  • Left 'Controls' panel: 'Home' returns to the Home Screen, 'App Switcher' switches between open apps, 'Lock' locks the phone, and 'Control Center' opens Wi-Fi/Airplane Mode.
  • Use the Up / Down / Left / Right directional arrows under 'SCROLL' to swipe smoothly through feeds or home screens.

2. Fast Password & Text Entry ('Type -> Send' Box)

  • Tap the text field on the iPhone screen so the blinking cursor appears.
  • On the right side of AliRemote under 'Type (MESSAGE)', paste or type your password, email, or bio.
  • Click 'Send' (or 'Send to clipboard')—it types straight into the phone with zero typos.

3. Bottom-Right 'Notes' Box & Right-Side 'Content' Upload Button

  • Bottom-Right 'Notes' Box: Every phone stores its iCloud Email, iCloud Password, Romanian SIM number (+40...), and Gmail here for instant copy-pasting.
  • Far-Right 'Content' (Photo Icon) Button: Click this icon on the right vertical toolbar whenever you need to upload cleaned Profile Pictures, Banners, Carousels, or Reels into the iPhone's Camera Roll.

4. Downloading & Arranging the 5 Apps in Exact Order

  • Open the Apple App Store on the iPhone and install these 5 apps in exact order: (1) Gmail -> (2) Facebook -> (3) Instagram -> (4) YouTube -> (5) X (Twitter).
  • If prompted for the Apple ID password, copy the iCloud password from the Notes box, paste into 'Type', click 'Send', and tap Sign In.
  • Once installed, press 'Home', long-press the app icons, and drag all 5 apps to the bottom of the Home Screen in exact left-to-right order: Gmail, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X.

Lesson 8 Lesson 8 — Hunting Outlier Reels (Views & Vibe Rules)

How to scan creator Reels tabs on Instagram, ignore flat 20k–80k baselines, and pick true 150k–437k+ outlier spikes that pass our strict solo/face/vibe rules.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE: Ignore flat 20k–80k view baselines—only clone clear outlier spikes (150k, 220k, 437k+ views) where the girl is solo, her face is visible, and the vibe is natural chill beauty.
GRANULAR WRITTEN SOP SHEET (PAIR WITH VIDEO OR DOWNLOAD PDF ABOVE)

1. Scanning View Counts for True Outliers

  • Open a high-quality real creator's Instagram Reels tab and look at her normal view baseline.
  • If all her videos sit around 20k to 80k views, skip those—fans didn't care enough to make them go viral.
  • Scroll until you spot a massive outlier spike that clearly stands out from the surrounding grid (e.g., 158k, 220k, 437k+ views).

2. The 3 Strict Filter Rules Before Copying Any Reel Link

  • RULE 1 — SOLO GIRL ONLY (No Random People): Watch the full clip first. If a random woman or man walks into the frame or stands in the background, skip it—the clone model may lock onto the wrong person.
  • RULE 2 — FACE CLEARLY VISIBLE: Skip clips where her face is hidden or turned away the whole time.
  • RULE 3 — CHILL LIFESTYLE/BEAUTY VIBE (No Comedy Skits): Skip talking-head comedy skits or gimmicky jokes (e.g., 'is that a wand in your pants'). Pick natural, chill clips where she is showing off her beauty/outfit to music or a short audio trend.
